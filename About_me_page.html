Josiah Krogman's Biography

He is currently pursuing an MS LIS degree at the University of Wisconsin-Madison and hopes to complete his degree by the fall of 2027. While attending school, he also works as assistant registrar and associate librarian. In his free time, he enjoys spending time with his family and playing music.

Mari Shishido's Biography

She is currently MS LIS student and originally from Japan. She has been working as a student assistant at Memorial Library for the Japanese Studies Librarian for more than a year. Before coming to the USA, she had worked as a distributor in Japan. She was in charge of sales, internal sales, and renewal sales for more than 10 years. The main products were the US company’s database and some tools for libraries.
